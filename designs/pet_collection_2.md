Plain generic text with no craft is the highest removal/low-visibility risk. Mitigation here: every design carries a hand-drawn spot illustration, a custom lockup, and a shared brand system.

## Brand system: "Well-Loved Household" (replaces the plain-text template)

Goal: every piece looks like a labeled item from a well-loved household (pet-care tag / vintage packaging), so the shop is recognizable and the designs are visibly crafted, not just fonts.

- Lockup: bold slab headline (Bowlby One or Alfa Slab One) + ONE hand-drawn spot illustration (uneven, linocut/marker-cut feel, flat fills, 1 to 2 colors) + a small "label line" set in a rounded ticket or pill outline (DM Sans or Fredoka, uppercase). Every design has these three parts in the same arrangement so the range reads as a set.
- Signature mark: a small stitched-tag style stamp "WELL-LOVED HOUSEHOLD" (tiny, 2.5 in wide) on the back neck of every piece (Printify back-neck/upper-back print; check per-provider availability). This gives brand recognition and looks like a real label.
- Shared inks on every design: cream #F4E9D0 or espresso #3E2A1E as the base text ink (depending on blank), plus one accent per design. Max 3 colors.
- Illustration rule: shapes are drawn with slightly imperfect, thick contour lines and visible cut/notch details (no perfect vector geometry). Purpose: warmer look, and it demonstrates original craft for Etsy's Creativity Standards. The owner should redraw or edit AI output by hand (trace in Illustrator/Procreate/Inkscape) rather than using raw output.
- Small-line rule: every design has a swappable label line (breed, name, "est." date), which supports personalization without custom art.
- Fonts (OFL on Google Fonts; verify): Bowlby One / Alfa Slab One (headline), Fredoka (labels), Caveat (hand notes), DM Sans (small caps), Fraunces (serif accent). Max 2 per design.
- Layout: headline max 4 lines, centered; art above or beside; label line below in a pill. No glows/shadows.
- Standard file spec unless noted: transparent PNG, sRGB, 300 DPI, on 3600 x 4800 canvas for full-front or trimmed to the stated size. Verify current Printify print area (about 12 x 16 in for 18000). Keep art 0.5 in inside edges.
- Pricing/positioning: $44.99 listing with a "Designed in-house, printed to order" line. The brand system, not the slogan alone, is what justifies price. Test $39.99 vs $44.99 on the first two listings if traffic is thin.

## Owner checklist (carried from v1, still applies)

1. AI disclosure per Etsy Creativity Standards; describe how the item was made ("Designed by [shop] with AI-assisted illustration, hand-edited; text and layout typeset by us"). Re-read current wording before listing.
2. Disclose Printify as production partner (listing field + shop settings).
3. Order samples: #1 (light), #5 (black), #10 (dark green), and one heather (#3 or #7).
4. Save font licenses, generator terms, prompts, and output records per design. Note AI-only art has limited US copyright protection; hand-edited art and the layout are your defensible part.
5. Trademark: run every slogan in tmsearch.uspto.gov (classes 25, 35, 18, 21) and Etsy exact-phrase search. Web-search "no hits" is not clearance.
6. Do not use brand names, breed club or kennel club names/logos, character names. Avoid "Doodle Dad" and "Tell Your Dog I Said Hi"-style phrases.
7. No medical/service-dog implication on #5.
8. Honest mockups, size chart, correct color names.
