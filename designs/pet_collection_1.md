## Collection concept

"Well-Loved Breed" is a 10-piece capsule of text-led crewnecks for pet parents who identify with a specific breed or personality (the golden that lives for a tennis ball, the reactive dog that needs space, the senior dog who has earned the good couch) rather than the crowded generic "dog mom" lane. Every design uses one shared template: a bold typeset headline, one small hand-drawn-style spot icon (paw, ball, loaf, heart), and a small secondary line, so a solo owner can swap breed/name in minutes with no photoreal art. Eight of ten designs are evergreen; #10 (Warm Feet) is the cold-weather/holiday-flavored piece and #7 (Slow Walks) and #6 (Second Chance) also work as cozy gift pieces for Q4. Because we are late for Q4 (Sept 28), list evergreen pieces first and push #10 and #6 as gifts. Tone: warm, dry-witty, never mean.

## Brand look guidelines

- Fonts (all free for commercial use under SIL Open Font License on Google Fonts; still re-verify each font page and download the files yourself before use): headline **Bowlby One** or **Alfa Slab One** (chunky, works at chest size); friendly display **Fredoka**; secondary/handwritten **Caveat**; clean small text **DM Sans**; retro serif option **Fraunces**. Pick max 2 fonts per design. Do not use fonts from "free" font sites with personal-use-only licenses.
- License reminder: OFL permits commercial use and embedding in artwork, but you may not sell the font file itself. Keep a folder with each font's license text and the download date. AI image outputs: check the generator's current commercial-use terms for your plan, and note copyrightability of purely AI-generated art is limited in the US, so you may have weak protection against copycats. Typeset text, layout, and your edits are the most defensible part.
- Layout system: headline block max 4 lines, centered, tight leading; small icon above or below; secondary line in a smaller size. Keep art simple with flat 1 to 3 solid colors so DTG stays crisp and no halftones are needed.
- Palette family: warm neutrals plus one accent per design. Prefer designs with no pure black-on-dark-blank contrast problems (see per-design inks).
- Blanks: Gildan 18000 colors that photograph and print well: Sand, Ash, Sport Grey, Dark Heather, Forest Green, Navy, Maroon, Black. Heather/Ash blanks show slightly muted inks (DTG on heathers looks a bit vintage); confirm with a sample. Dark blanks require a white underbase (Printify DTG handles automatically, but small fine lines can look rough).
- Standard file spec unless overridden: transparent PNG, sRGB, 300 DPI, 3600 x 4800 px canvas (12 x 16 in) for full-front; chest designs are still exported on the same canvas or trimmed to their true size (e.g. 3000 x 3000 px = 10 x 10 in). Printify's max front print area for 18000 is about 12 x 16 in (verify in the current Printify product page, values change). Keep art 0.5 in inside edges, no semi-transparent glows or soft shadows (DTG prints them as muddy fringes), leave no stray pixels.

## Owner checklist (do before listing)

1. AI disclosure: Etsy requires you to state how the item was made. Set the listing's creativity/"made by" field to "I did" with AI-assisted design disclosed, and add a plain sentence in the description: "Designed by [shop] with the help of AI image tools; text and layout typeset by us." Confirm current wording in Etsy's Creativity Standards (rules change; check the Seller Handbook).
2. Print partner disclosure: state that items are made to order and printed by a print partner (Printify) and add that to listing "production partner" field. Add Printify production partner details in Etsy shop settings.
3. Order one sample of at least designs 1, 5, and 10 (dark blank + light blank + heather) to check ink appearance, stitching, and print placement before launching all ten.
4. License checks: save font licenses (OFL); check your image generator's commercial terms and save a dated screenshot; keep prompt + output records per design.
5. Trademark: the searches below used general web search only (USPTO tmsearch was not queried directly). Before launch, run each slogan in tmsearch.uspto.gov (word mark, classes 25 clothing, 35, 18, 21) and search Etsy for the exact phrase. "No hits" here means "nothing found by web search," not "cleared."
6. Do not use brand names or breed club logos, "AKC," "Kennel Club," character names, or lyrics in titles/tags. Avoid "Doodle Dad" phrase (registered mark found).
7. Do not claim breed traits as medical or training claims; #5 (reactive) should not imply a service-dog or medical status.
8. Mockups: use Printify mockups honestly; add a size chart; order-check color naming.

## 1. Golden Habit

- Exact text: **GOLDEN HEART. / TENNIS BALL HABIT.** Small line under: "golden retriever household" (swap "household" with "mom"/"dad"/"crew").
- Target buyer: Golden retriever owners (women 28 to 55, plus gift-givers for them). Highest-demand breed on Etsy.
- Style: Bowlby One headline in two stacked lines; small drawn tennis ball with motion arc between the lines; secondary line in Caveat. Rounded, sunny, friendly.
- Sweatshirt: Sand. Ink: #B8741A (gold), #4A2E12 (dark brown text), #C7D64A (ball accent).
- Placement: full front, 11 in wide x 9 in tall, centered 3 in below the collar. File: 3300 x 2700 px at 300 DPI, transparent PNG (or place on the standard 3600 x 4800 canvas).
- Image prompt: "Simple flat vector-style illustration of a single tennis ball with three curved motion lines, hand-drawn look, thick uniform outlines, flat colors only in mustard gold, brown and lime yellow, isolated on a pure transparent-looking plain white background, centered, no shadow, no gradient, no text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark, no dog, no photorealism, screen-print style."
- Variant: Swap the breed word and the ball icon (Lab: "LAB LOGIC. / TENNIS BALL HABIT." with a duck decoy icon; goldendoodle: replace "GOLDEN" with "DOODLE" but avoid the phrase "Doodle Dad").
- IP check: PASS (low confidence). The original wording "Tennis Ball Obsessed Golden Retriever" exists on Amazon and Etsy ("Tennis Ball is Life"), so I avoided that phrase. Searching "Golden Hearted Tennis Ball Obsessed" and "Golden Heart / Tennis Ball Habit" found no trademark or exact-phrase merch. Theme is crowded, phrase seems distinct. Fix if collision later: reword to "Golden Heart. Tennis Ball Problem."
