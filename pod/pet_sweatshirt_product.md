## Etsy fees (verified by several 2026 fee-guide snippets and Etsy's fee policy page in results)
- $0.20 listing (per listing; treated as $0.20 per sale here, conservative). 6.5% transaction fee on item price (+shipping if charged).
  Payment processing 3% + $0.25 (US). Offsite Ads: 15% (shops under $10k trailing-12-month), 12% and mandatory above $10k.
  Opt-out is allowed under $10k (per guides; confirm the toggle in Shop Manager > Marketing > Offsite Ads).

## Print-file spec (Printify DTG; confirm in the Printify mockup uploader) [UNVERIFIED specifics]
- PNG with transparent background, sRGB, 300 DPI; design at up to ~4500 x 5400 px (15 x 18 in) is Printify's common guidance;
  keep art within the provider's front print area (~12 x 16 in) and center 1.5-3 in below the collar.
- Avoid pure white fills on white shirts; supply a light-ink variant for dark garments (black/navy) and a dark-ink variant for gray/white.
- No fine hairlines under ~1 pt, no semi-transparent gradients (DTG gray haze on dark shirts).
- Keep files under Printify's upload limit (about 100 MB) [UNVERIFIED].

## Before launch checklist
1. In Printify app: open Gildan 18000, list all 19 providers with price, ship (US), production days, quality score, color/size list; confirm Monster Digital numbers above. Record in a table.
2. Add to cart with real sizes (S and 2XL) to see true shipping; check for the holiday surcharge.
3. After Oct 15 read the Network Fulfillment Status / Holiday Hub cutoff for the chosen provider and put an order-by date in listings.
4. Turn off Offsite Ads; enable free shipping at $44.99+ price (set Etsy shipping profile to free, US only); set processing time 3-5 days.
5. Connect Printify to Etsy; check "AI-generated design" disclosure and Printify as production partner in the listing (Etsy requirement).
6. Trademark/USPTO check of slogans; check breed names aren't trademarked phrases.
7. Order and inspect samples BEFORE running any ads; don't promote until the sample passes.
8. Size chart in listing photos (Gildan 18000 runs true-to-size/loose; unverified).
9. Set Printify to manual approval of orders if wanting to catch mistakes; consider disabling Printify Choice if it routes to providers with poor scores.
