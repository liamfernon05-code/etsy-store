# Market comparison: top pet-parent apparel on Etsy vs our 10 designs (2026-09-28)

## 1. Method and evidence limits (read first)

Method: about 18 WebSearch queries (sub-theme by sub-theme: golden, husky, dachshund, reactive, foster/rescue, senior, cat, Christmas/holiday, general dog mom, plus Comfort Colors vs Gildan and Etsy trend articles). Etsy and its API are blocked from this sandbox, so no Etsy page was fetched. No proxy bypass, no scraping.

What the snippets DID show: listing titles, listing URLs, some blank/fabric wording from listing copy, personalization/embroidery wording, and which Etsy market pages exist.

What the snippets did NOT show (important, do not over-read this file):
- NO listing price was quoted in any snippet. Price bands below are therefore UNVERIFIED.
- NO review count, star rating or "Bestseller" badge was quoted for any listing. Exceptions: one snippet says a custom reactive-dog listing has "an average rating of 4.8 or higher" (search-engine paraphrase, count not shown), and one says the "Adopt Senior Dogs" seller is a Star Seller. Several search-summary sentences call listings "bestselling"; that text came from Etsy market-page titles ("Some of the bestselling ... available on Etsy include ..."), which is Etsy boilerplate, not a badge on the listing. I do not treat it as proof of sales.
- Sales counts per listing are not public. Nothing here measures demand.
- The search summarizer occasionally garbled blank names (e.g. calls Gildan 18000 "Softstyle"; a "Comfort Colors" crewneck title carries a 50/50 8 oz spec, which is Gildan 18000's spec). Blank labels are therefore "as stated in title/copy", and spec-based inferences are marked as inferred.
- My queries partly contained "Comfort Colors", which biases the blank counts toward Comfort Colors. Percentages in section 3 are a rough skew indicator on a small non-random sample, not market shares.
- Third-party seller-tool blogs (eRank, EverBee, Sale Samurai) surfaced only as titles; no numeric data from them was visible.

Bottom line on evidence: this is a qualitative map of what page one looks like, not a measured ranking of best sellers.

## 3. Patterns (with the limits above)

- Price bands: UNVERIFIED, no price seen. Two loose signals only: Etsy's own "under $50" and free-shipping filters on dog-mom pages, and a snippet that some Comfort Colors sweatshirt sellers charge "$80+" (search-summary wording, unattributed). My unsourced prior, not evidence: POD dog-mom crewnecks mostly sit around $30 to $60, personalized and embroidered higher. Do not price off this; pull real data (section 6).
- Personalized vs not: among about 30 individual listings, 7 are explicitly personalized (#4, 7, 8, 10, 21, 34, 36) which is about 23%, plus 1 embroidered (#38), about 27% with either. This understates their weight: every Etsy "bestselling" line quoted on the general dog-mom pages was personalized or embroidered (#39). Non-personalized examples are mostly text or single-illustration jokes.
- Blank as stated: Comfort Colors named in 7 listings (#1-5, 9, 32; #17 offers both). Gildan 18000 named in 3 (#15, 16, 20; #17 offers it). Gildan-type wording inferred in 4 (#7, 12, 26, 34). About 19 of 30 have no blank stated. Among the 10 with a stated blank, roughly 70% Comfort Colors and 30% Gildan; among all 30 it is about 23% CC named vs 10% Gildan named. My query wording biased this toward CC. Takeaway: CC is heavily advertised in titles (it is a search keyword), Gildan 18000 listings exist and sell in the dachshund, reactive and senior lanes, and Gildan sellers rarely put the brand in the title.
- Formats: tees are as common as sweatshirts in themed lanes (golden, husky, cat, foster), so buyers of these themes often buy the tee; a sweatshirt is the colder-season variant.
- Slogan styles recurring: identity labels ("Golden Mama", "Weenie Mom", "Foster Mama", "Cat Mom Club", "Dog Mom Era"), club/"era" formats, "Easily Distracted by X" (dachshund and cat both), one-line jokes ("World's Greatest Spare Human", "Foster Fail", "Cats Welcome People Tolerated"), advocacy ("Adopt Senior Dogs", "Reactive Dog Awareness"). Multi-line wry full-sentence jokes like ours are rarer in what I saw, but I could not see thumbnails or sales.
- Style: retro/vintage, varsity, groovy/hippie, gingham and floral illustration dominate over bold slab text.

Blunt weaknesses that apply to all 10:
- Zero reviews and no shop history. Every example I found sits in a lane where established sellers carry reviews we could not see but must assume exist.
- No personalization. The only listing groups Etsy itself labels "bestselling" on the dog-mom pages are personalized or embroidered.
- Gildan 18000 at $44.99. Comfort Colors is the blank most often named in titles. Buyers who search "Comfort Colors" will not find our listings. In the dachshund, reactive and senior lanes, Gildan is used by comparable sellers, so the penalty is lane-dependent, largest in golden/husky/foster.
- Our price is at the high end of what a Gildan fleece POD sweatshirt commonly costs (my prior, unverified); the price is justified only if photos and copy look premium.
- Bold multi-line slab text is a thumbnail-friendly but less "cozy" look than retro/gingham/vintage styles that dominate page one.

## 5. Recommendations

Launch first (all evidence-limited):
1. #5 Reactive (niche with few visible humorous options; Gildan is normal; add name/pronoun variants). Hold only for the IP phrase check already in our file.
2. #6 Foster (gift buyers; unique phrase). Also list a "Foster Fail" keyword variant in tags/subtitle without leading with it.
3. #4 Maximum Dog (dachshund lane accepts Gildan).
4. #10 Heated by Dog (only if it can be live within days; seasonal).
5. #3 Husky, then #7, #1, #9. Hold #2 and #8.

Price: test $39.99 to $42.99 for the first 30 days for a zero-review shop (unit economics from our pod file: net about $15.15 at $39.99 with $5.35 shipping, versus $19.68 at $44.99); return to $44.99 once reviews exist. This is a judgment, not a market fact, since competitor prices were not visible.

Blank and personalization:
- Add a single personalization field (pet name in the label pill, or year adopted) on #1, #3, #5, #6, #7. The evidence that personalization is the top-seller feature is Etsy's own market-page wording plus the listing mix, not sales data.
- Offer Comfort Colors 1566 as a second variant on the golden, husky and foster designs (from $31.29 per our pod notes) at a higher price; use "Comfort Colors" in those titles only once actually stocked. Keep Gildan for reactive, dachshund and senior.
- Confirm Monster Digital colors; several planned blanks (Sand, Forest, Maroon, Ash) are unverified per our files.

Top 5 conversion levers:
1. Real sample photos on a person, first image with the joke readable at thumbnail size.
2. Personalization option (name/year) on breed and rescue designs.
3. Breed/niche keywords first in titles (husky mom sweatshirt, reactive dog mom, foster mom crewneck, dachshund mom sweatshirt) since page one uses identity keywords, not our slogans.
4. Comfort Colors variant for lanes where it is the visible norm.
5. Early reviews: friends, family and a small discount on the first 10 orders; without reviews, ranking and trust both stall.
